## Changelog 0.8.0

### Added
- Added the Ping Wheel.
- Added Create mixing and filling for apple cider, melon juice, hot cocoa, and 13 soup and stew fluids.
- Added stonecutting variants for dacite and white dacite.
- Added BadOptimizations, Dynamic FPS, and NoisiumForked.
- Added Artifacts and Beltborne Lanterns.
- Added VMP serverside.

### Changed
- Moved Master Cutter stonecutting recipes to KubeJS.
- Recreated Better Than Mending and Grass Seeds behavior in KubeJS, with support for modded dirt.
- Enabled entity activation in ServerCore.

### Updated
- Updated Plushie Friends from 1.0.0 to 1.0.1.
- Updated Syrup Library from 0.5.0 to 0.5.1.

### Removed
- Removed Better Than Mending, Grass Seeds, Does It Tick, and Chunk Activity Tracker.
- Removed the Master Cutter datapack files.
