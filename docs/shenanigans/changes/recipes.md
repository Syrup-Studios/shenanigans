# Recipe changes

## Copper progression

Copper swords, tools, and armor use copper ingots in their recipes and repairs. Copper hammers and excavators also use copper in place of stone. See [tools and armor](../tools%20%26%20armor/README.md) for details.

Smelting copper, iron, or gold ore takes 60 seconds in a furnace or 5 seconds in a blast furnace at normal game speed. Smelting raw copper, raw iron, raw gold, or Nether gold ore takes 60 seconds in a furnace.

## Food and mob drops

Furnace smelting recipes are removed for items tagged as food. Raw-food tooltips point to the smoker or campfire. Check each dish's recipe for its cooking method.

You can craft some mob drops:

| Output | Ingredients |
| --- | --- |
| 1 gunpowder | 1 flint |
| 1 ender pearl | 8 obsidian, 1 gold ingot |
| 1 blaze rod | 6 quartz, 1 lava bucket |
| 1 ghast tear | 8 quartz, 1 water bucket |
| 1 slime ball | 4 cactus, 4 lily pads, 1 water bucket |

Smoking one rotten flesh gives one leather.

## Other useful recipes

- Craft a map with eight paper around a compass. The compass stays in the grid. Crafting a filled map makes a regular map and clears its explored data.
- Craft a spyglass with two copper ingots and one glass pane.
- Stonecut one amethyst block into four amethyst shards.
- Turn one wool block into two string by crafting or Create milling. Create crushing gives four string and has a 50% chance to give one more.
- Craft tuff with one gravel, one flint, and one stone crafting material. Create crushing recipes for tuff are removed.
- The stonecutter has recipes for many pack blocks, including related slabs, stairs, and walls.
- Eggs, brown and blue eggs, and ender pearls stack to 64.
