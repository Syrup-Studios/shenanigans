# Tools and Armor

Copper swords, pickaxes, axes, shovels, hoes, and armor use copper ingots in their recipes and repairs. Copper tools have the same mining limits as stone tools.

Copper hammers and excavators use copper in their recipes. Their area settings are in the [hammer and excavator table](../changes/mods/onlyhammersandexcavators/README.md).

To repair a damaged item with Mending, crouch and right-click while holding it. This spends XP. You need more than 2 XP.

Immersive Armors adds more armor options. See [recipe changes](../changes/recipes.md) for copper smelting times and other recipes.
