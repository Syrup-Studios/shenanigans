# Farming & Food

Farmer's Delight, Nature's Delight, Crops Love Rain, and Let's Do mods add farming and cooking options. Let's Do includes Bakery, Brewery, Candlelight, Farm & Charm, Meadow, and Vinery.

Use wheat seeds on vanilla dirt, Oh The Biomes We've Gone lush dirt, and Regions Unexplored peat or silt dirt to turn them into grass blocks. Use seeds on these grass blocks to grow short grass, then on short grass to grow tall grass. Seed use consumes one seed outside Creative mode. A blocked tall-grass attempt can still consume a seed.

Furnace smelting recipes are removed for items tagged as food. Raw-food tooltips point to the smoker or campfire. Check each dish's recipe for its cooking method.

See [recipe changes](../changes/recipes.md) for alternative food and mob-drop recipes.
